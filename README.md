# RHollins_Portfolio
Hello, my name is Robert Hollins. I am a sophomore Posse Scholar at Connecticut College and I intend to get my degree in Computer Science. I'm specifically passionate about cybersecurity, issues when it comes to digital equity and creative technologies in the arts. I'm also a Sociology double major on the Data, Information, and Society Pahtway here at Conn as I'm extremely passionate about survey collecting, and understanding the social aspects of how technology and people intersect.



This most recent summer I worked under Prof. Christine Chung as a research assistant exploring voting systems and computational social choice, where I analyze real election data and model how different ranked‑choice algorithms behave in practice. My work blends computer science and civic impact two things I'm extremely passionate about. 



The two projects you see In this portfolio are a text-based dungeon-crawler where you can receive multiple endings depending on your choices, written in Java. The other is a mock database for one of my favorite grocery store chains Seafood City, which specializes in Filipino items written in SQL with the final graphical user-interface written in Pyhon.
